🌟 Introduction

Indian Knowledge System (IKS) represents the knowledge, traditions, ideas and practices that developed in India over thousands of years.

It covers many different areas including education, philosophy, mathematics, science, medicine, culture, literature and art.

📖 1. Meaning of Indian Knowledge System

Indian Knowledge System includes various forms of knowledge developed through observation, experience, research and learning.

💡 Important

IKS connects traditional knowledge with human life, society, nature and values.

⭐ 2. Important Features

  • Long historical tradition
  • Knowledge from different fields
  • Importance of practical knowledge
  • Connection between knowledge and life
  • Importance of values and ethics
  • Respect for nature and environment

🎯 3. Importance of Indian Knowledge System

The Indian Knowledge System helps us understand India's intellectual, cultural and scientific heritage.

It also provides an opportunity to understand traditional knowledge and connect it with modern education, research and society.

🔍 4. Examples

  • Yoga
  • Ayurveda
  • Indian Mathematics
  • Astronomy
  • Indian Philosophy
  • Ancient Education System
